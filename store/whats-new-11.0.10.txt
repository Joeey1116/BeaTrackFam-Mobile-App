## What's new in this version
- Order History is here: orders you place show up instantly, and you can sign in with your email to see every order with live tracking
- Full order details in the app — items, totals, shipping status, and tracking links when your package is on its way
- Need to cancel? Request a cancellation right from your order (before it ships)
- Promo codes at checkout — add up to 2 and they sync straight to your order
- Fixed sign-in profile loading and checkout with older cart items
- Order History shows which email you're signed in with, your real order statuses (including cancelled), and a sign-out button
- Items that are only on our website now say so right on their page, so checkout never hits a dead end
